Hexa: privacy policy

Applies to the Hexa app for Android (package store.sombrim.hexa). Last updated 6 October 2026.

In short

Hexa keeps what you put into it on your own device. It has no accounts and no Hexa server. The developer does not receive, see, sell or share your information.

What Hexa stores, and where

  • Your panels, tiles and their content (names, notes, to-do lists, links, pictures you add, colours and layout).
  • Your ICE (in case of emergency) details, if you choose to enter them: for example your name, blood type, allergies and conditions, an emergency contact, date of birth, and any rows you add.
  • ICE details for a child, if a parent or guardian chooses to create a child's code.
  • Your six-digit lock code, kept in a scrambled form.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere and is not backed up by Hexa.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device unless you send it yourself:

  • QR codes. A QR code that Hexa makes contains the details you entered for it, as plain text. Anyone who scans the code, on your lock screen or on a printed sticker, can read those details. Put in only what a helper or finder needs.
  • Print and Share. When you print a code or use Share, your device's own print or share function takes over, and the file goes wherever you send it.
  • Share my Hexa. This makes a file of your layout and tile content for another Hexa user. Anything you have padlocked, and all ICE details and codes, are left out. You choose who receives the file.
  • Links. If you tap a link you saved in a tile, it opens in your browser or in the app it belongs to, which have their own privacy policies.
  • Email the developer. If you choose to email the developer, your email app opens and you decide what to send.

What Hexa does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone. Hexa asks for no special permissions.

Children

Hexa is meant for adults. A parent or guardian may enter a child's emergency details to print a code for the child. Those details stay on the adult's device, like everything else.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete items inside Hexa at any time. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling Hexa, removes everything. Because Hexa holds no copy, the developer cannot recover it for you.

Security

Hexa is opened with a six-digit code. Your information is as safe as your device: keep your device locked. Your ICE code is shown on Hexa's lock screen on purpose, so a first responder can scan it.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when subscriptions or syncing between devices are added, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
